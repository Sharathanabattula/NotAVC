# NotAVC — Color Rules (locked, follow in everything)

**Anchor: Crimson Depth `#710014`** — HSL 349° / 100% / 22%. Never change or substitute the anchor.

The palette is a **monochromatic + analogous-warm** scheme, applied scientifically off the RYB color wheel:

1. **Monochromatic depth** — hierarchy comes from shades/tints of the *same* hue (349°), not new colors. Fills: `#710014`. Tints: `rgba(113,0,20,.08/.12/.18)`. On dark backgrounds crimson is too dark to read as text → use the lighter same-hue tint **`#D8495F`**.
2. **Analogous warm partners** (hue red→orange, ~349°–30°) — the only decorative accents allowed beside crimson: warm-sand/bronze **`#B38F6F`** (28°), rust, aged-gold `#C9A470`.
3. **Tinted neutrals, never dead grey** — neutrals carry warmth so they belong to the family: canvas `#F2F1ED`, muted taupe **`#7E7264`**, on-dark muted `#9C8D84`. Do **not** put pure `#888` grey next to crimson — it reads as a clash.
4. **One reserved COOL accent** — info blue `#3B8BD4`, for data/"positive/live" only. Never decorative.
5. **Verdict semantics** (teardowns only): coral `#E8593C` (red-orange, post-mortem), amber `#F2A623` (caution), info blue (live bet).
6. **No green.** Red's true complement on the wheel is green — deliberately avoided. Harmony comes from monochrome depth + analogous warmth instead.

**Two-ink discipline:** ink = what he wrote, crimson = what he corrected. Crimson never decorates; max 3 crimson elements per frame.

**Adding ANY new color** — it must be one of: (a) a same-hue crimson tint/shade, (b) an analogous warm within ~±35° of 349°, or (c) the single reserved cool data-accent. Nothing else.
