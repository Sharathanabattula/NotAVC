# NotAVC — Content Strategy (The Daily Platter System)

**Owner:** Sharath Chandra Anabattula
**System:** One "platter" per day. Each platter = everything needed to post across LinkedIn + Instagram for that day, pre-written in brand voice, ready to paste. No 30-day countdown, no 24-hour pressure. The platter waits for you; the streak is the system's job, not yours.

---

## The operating rhythm

| Day | Pillar | LinkedIn format | Instagram format |
|-----|--------|-----------------|------------------|
| Mon | VC/PE concept explained | Text post (<130 words) | Carousel (8 slides) |
| Tue | Company teardown | Teardown post + quote card | Carousel (teardown format) |
| Wed | Founder/CEO story | Story post | Reel (60s, faceless) |
| Thu | Market analysis | Data post | Carousel (data-led) |
| Fri | Learning journal | "3 things I learned" | Reel (journal format) |
| Sat | Startup idea + analysis | Back-of-envelope post | Carousel or Story poll |
| Sun | OFF / batch prep | — | Story poll only (optional) |

Rules carried from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md v2.0 — these are non-negotiable:

- Every piece gets an episode number: `EP.001`, `EP.002`… shared across platforms for the same topic.
- **Quality gate (all 7 must pass):** hook stops the scroll · at least one specific number · student/learning angle present · under word limit · no banned words · a 22-year-old gets it · a VC would respect it.
- **Banned words:** thrilled, honored, journey, game-changer, leverage, unlock, empower, delve, navigate, foster, seamless, robust, holistic, elevate.
- **Hashtags:** `#NotAVC` mandatory + rotate 3 from the bank. Max 4 total. Max 1 emoji per post.
- **Voice:** raw, first person, chai-side explanation, admit gaps, end with a question or honest statement.

## Platform playbook

### LinkedIn (primary growth engine)
- Post window: **8:30–10:00 AM IST** weekdays (MBA/professional audience checks before class/work).
- Structure: 1-line hook → white space → short punchy paragraphs → one Space-Mono-style number → closing question. Under 130 words for journal posts; teardowns can run to ~200.
- First comment: drop the source/data link there, never in the post body (LinkedIn suppresses external links).
- Fridays: the journal post is the relationship-builder. It can "fail" on reach — it wins on trust.

### Instagram (brand surface)
- Post window: **12:30–1:30 PM IST** or **7:30–9:00 PM IST**.
- Carousels from `assets/carousel-templates.html` (4:5, 1080×1350). Reels from `assets/faceless-reels.html` (9:16).
- Slide 1 = hook only. Slide N-1 = "my take as a student." Slide N = CTA + brand bar.
- Caption: first 125 chars must stand alone (that's the fold). Repeat the ONE number in the caption.

## The hook bank (rotate, never repeat within 14 days)

1. "[Company] raised ₹[X]Cr. Here's what nobody's asking."
2. "This one number explains why [company] [won/imploded]."
3. "I didn't understand [term] until last Tuesday. Here it is in 40 seconds."
4. "Every MBA student gets this wrong about [topic]."
5. "[Big number] sounds impressive. It isn't. Here's why."
6. "A VC said no to [company] at ₹[X]Cr. It's now worth ₹[Y]Cr. What did everyone miss?"
7. "My professor skipped this. The term sheet didn't."
8. "[Founder] made one decision in [year] that [outcome]. Nobody talks about it."

## The platter format (what lands in your calendar every day)

Each platter is one file / one Airtable row containing:

1. **Episode number + pillar + date**
2. **LinkedIn post** — final copy, paste-ready
3. **Instagram carousel** — slide-by-slide text mapped to the carousel template
4. **Reel script** — HOOK/SETUP/CORE/OVEN/CLOSE with timings (when the day calls for a reel)
5. **Caption + hashtags** — pre-checked against hashtag rules
6. **Fact-check list** — every number in the post, listed, so you can verify in 2 minutes before posting
7. **Status field** — `Drafted → Reviewed → Posted` (you flip it; nothing auto-publishes)

## The one rule that matters

The platter is a *draft written in your voice*, not a ghostwriter's final word. Read it, make one edit so it's yours, verify the numbers, post. If a day slips, the platter rolls forward — episodes are sequential, not date-locked.
