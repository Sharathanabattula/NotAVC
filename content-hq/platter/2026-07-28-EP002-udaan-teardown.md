## LinkedIn post (paste-ready)

Udaan "raised $160M" last week. Read the structure before you clap.

The round is a mix: new equity, fresh debt, and old debt converted into equity.

Why that matters: debt-to-equity conversion isn't new money. It's old lenders accepting shares because getting repaid in cash looked worse. The headline number counts it anyway.

Udaan has been here before — B2B e-commerce burns cash on thin trade margins, and earlier rounds already came with steep valuation resets.

The question I can't answer from the press release: how much of the $160M is actual new capital hitting the bank account? That number wasn't in any headline I found.

As a student, this is my new rule: every "raised $X" headline gets one follow-up — raised how?

What's the most creative round structure you've seen?
