## LinkedIn post (paste-ready)

At 50, Falguni Nayar left a managing director seat at Kotak to sell lipstick online. Everyone in the room thought she was late.

Context: 2012. E-commerce meant fighting Flipkart and Amazon on discounts. Beauty was a "small category."

The decision: don't fight on price. Build trust in a category where fakes are the customer's biggest fear. Curated inventory, authenticity guarantees, content before commerce.

Outcome: Nykaa IPO'd in 2021 — one of the only Indian consumer-tech listings that was actually profitable at IPO.

The lesson I'm taking: she didn't find a gap in the market. She found a gap in trust. Those are different searches.

What "too late" market would you still enter today?
