## LinkedIn post (paste-ready)

Indian startups raised ~$820M in July. June was $1.91B. But the number that caught me is smaller than both.

$209M — last week's total. And for once, AI wasn't the biggest theme. Manufacturing, aerospace, enterprise software, and healthtech led the week.

Three data points from July:
Week of Jul 18: $346M across 20 deals — up 331% year-on-year. Udaan and Emergent (India's newest AI unicorn) drove it.
Week of Jul 25: $209M, deeptech and manufacturing on top.
Full month: roughly $820M, less than half of June.

One month isn't a trend. But capital rotating from AI hype to factories and hardware is exactly the kind of shift that looks obvious only in hindsight.

I'm watching August for confirmation. Am I reading too much into one week?
