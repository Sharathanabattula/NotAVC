## LinkedIn post (paste-ready, under 130 words)

3 things I learned this week, studying VC as an MBA student:

1. Liquidation preferences matter more than valuation. A ₹500Cr exit can pay a founder ₹0. The term sheet is the deal; the headline is marketing.

2. "How much did they raise?" is the wrong question. "What's the contribution margin per order?" is the right one. Zepto taught me that — or rather, the absence of that number did.

3. I got something wrong: I assumed profitable IPOs were normal. Then I checked. Nykaa is the exception, not the rule.

One honest admission: a week ago I couldn't have read a preference stack. Documenting anyway.

What did you unlearn this week?
