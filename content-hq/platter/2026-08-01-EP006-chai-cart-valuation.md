## LinkedIn post (paste-ready)

I valued the chai cart outside my campus like a VC would. It embarrassed some startups.

Back of the envelope:
~400 cups/day × ₹15 = ₹6,000 daily revenue. Margins after milk, gas, and rent? Roughly 50%. Call it ₹9L profit a year. Zero CAC — the queue IS the marketing. Retention: daily. Churn: exams.

At a small-business multiple of 3–4x profit, that cart is worth ~₹30L. Real cash, real moat (the location), no burn.

Now the VC lens: it's a great business and a terrible venture investment. It can't 100x. No fund returns a portfolio on chai.

That's the actual lesson: VCs don't fund good businesses. They fund possible monopolies.

What's the best non-fundable business you know?
