# Template — Instagram carousel (4:5, use assets/carousel-templates.html)

```
SLIDE 1  — HOOK. ≤10 words + episode number. Nothing else.
SLIDE 2  — WHAT. The company/concept in one line a 22-year-old gets.
SLIDE 3  — THE NUMBER. One metric, huge, Space Mono. One-line label.
SLIDE 4  — WHY IT MATTERS. 2–3 short lines.
SLIDE 5  — THE MECHANISM. The how. Analogy slide if abstract.
SLIDE 6  — THE VC LENS. Why investors said yes (or should have said no).
SLIDE 7  — MY TAKE. "As a student, here's what I actually think."
SLIDE 8  — CLOSE. Question + NotAVC brand bar.
```

Caption: first 125 characters must stand alone and repeat THE number.
Then 2–3 lines of context. #NotAVC + 3 rotating tags. Max 1 emoji.
Post window: 12:30–1:30 PM or 7:30–9:00 PM IST.
