# Template — LinkedIn post

```
[HOOK — one line, ≤12 words, a number or a tension. No "I'm excited to share."]

[CONTEXT — 1–2 short lines. Why this matters today.]

[CORE — 3–5 short paragraphs or a tight list.
 One idea per paragraph. One number minimum, written plainly: ₹18,300Cr, not "a lot".
 One Indian-context analogy if the concept is abstract.]

[THE STUDENT ANGLE — one honest line: what I got wrong / didn't know / am watching.]

[CLOSE — a question or an honest statement. Never a CTA to "follow for more."]

#NotAVC #[rotate] #[rotate] #[rotate]
```

Rules: under 130 words for journal posts, ≤200 for teardowns. Max 4 hashtags, max 1 emoji.
Link (if any) goes in the first comment, never the body.
Post window: 8:30–10:00 AM IST weekdays.
