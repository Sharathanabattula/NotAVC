Cap table
A spreadsheet showing who owns what percentage of the company — founders, investors, employee option pool.

Think of it like the seating chart at a wedding, except the seats are equity and the venue is a company worth ₹500Cr.

Why it matters: every funding round dilutes existing shareholders. If you don't track it, you wake up one day owning 8% of the company you built.
